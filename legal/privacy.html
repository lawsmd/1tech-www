Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-05-14.

What we collect

1tech.us is a static website. The hosting provider records standard server access information (IP address, timestamp, user-agent, the page that was requested) when you visit. That data is retained according to the hosting provider's own policy and is used by us only to diagnose problems if the site stops working.

If you submit our contact form, you'll be asked for your name, your preferred contact information, and a message. That form is processed by a third-party service called Web3Forms, which forwards the submission to our inbox. We use that information only to reply to you.

What we don't collect

We do not run analytics, set cookies, or include tracking pixels of any kind. We don't collect anything beyond what you choose to send through the contact form or by email.

Third parties

Two third parties may see information about your visit or your message: the hosting provider for 1tech.us (server access logs) and Web3Forms (anything you put into the contact form). Their handling of that data is governed by their own respective terms of service.

Contact

If you have a question about what this site collects or does not collect, email hello@1tech.us.

Changes

If we ever change the privacy posture above — for example, by adding analytics, a contact form, or a third-party widget — we'll update this page and the "last updated" date.
