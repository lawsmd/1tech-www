What we do

1tech takes on custom software, consulting, and training engagements for small local businesses across Southwest Virginia.

  • Custom internal tools — intake, scheduling, billing, reporting — for small businesses.
  • Special focus in routine task automation, and bookkeeping and financing solutions.
  • Custom integrations between the tools you already use.
  • Website builds and revamps.
  • Training and consulting for teams adopting new software.
  • Training and consulting on AI and large language models, for teams that want to use or learn about them.

If your situation doesn't fit cleanly into one of those, get in touch and we'll talk it through.

How we work

Four steps from first contact to delivery.

  1. Initial consultation. A free conversation about what you need, followed by a free estimate. No commitment either way.
  2. Paid discovery. A short paid phase where we work out the details — what's being built, what it'll cost, how long it'll take — and produce a written statement of work that you own whether or not you engage us further.
  3. Build. We work in cycles, with regular check-ins on a cadence we agree on together. You see progress as it happens and you can redirect.
  4. Handoff and support. You receive documentation and source. We're available for follow-on work and ongoing support if you need it.
